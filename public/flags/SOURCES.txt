Flags that carry writing, as the complete official artwork.

Each file is the Wikimedia Commons original, downloaded on 2026-11-18 and stored
byte for byte: not minified, no path simplified, nothing edited. .gitattributes
turns line-ending conversion off for this folder so the hashes below hold on
every checkout. src/lib/countryFlags.test.mjs checks them.

To update a flag: download the file again from its URL, replace it unmodified,
record the new sha256 here and in src/lib/countryFlags.test.mjs, then open it
enlarged (900 px or wider) and check that the writing is complete.
